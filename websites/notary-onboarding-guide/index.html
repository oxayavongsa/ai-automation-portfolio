    Register for and take the state exam

    You

    Live Scan fingerprints

    With us

    After you pass, the state mails your fingerprint instructions. Bring the form to us. Must be done within one year of your exam.

    You

    You

    Oath of office + starter kit

    With us

    File your oath and bond with the County Clerk

    With us or you

    Must be filed within 30 days of your commission start date. We mail it overnight or certified, or you mail it yourself.
